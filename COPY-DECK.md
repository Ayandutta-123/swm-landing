## FAQ

**H2:** Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. How is IoT smart waste management different from fixed collection schedules?**  
A: Fixed schedules send trucks whether bins are empty or overflowing. HyperThings triggers collection from real-time fill levels — so crews go where demand is, not where the calendar says.

**Q2. What connectivity do the bin sensors use?**  
A: Sensors transmit over LoRa, NB-IoT, or GSM — chosen to match city coverage, density, and gateway strategy. Data lands on the HyperThings IoT Platform for dashboards, alerts, and routing.

**Q3. Ultrasonic vs radar — which sensor should we deploy?**  
A: Ultrasonic suits open measurement across many bin types and sizes, with ~5-year replaceable batteries. Radar measures through plastic walls/screens, supports non-invasive hazardous-waste use cases, GNSS GPS, and FOTA updates.

**Q4. What alerts does the platform send?**  
A: Full-bin, fire, and tilt alerts via dashboard plus e-mail/SMS notification paths — so supervisors and drivers act before overflows become citizen complaints.

**Q5. Can it integrate with existing fleet or municipal systems?**  
A: Yes. The architecture supports fleet management software, fleet dispatching, and 3rd-party integrations alongside the Waste Management App on HyperThings.

**Q6. How does route optimization save cost?**  
A: Live fill data feeds itinerary planning so vehicles skip empty or half-filled bins, compress routes, and reduce fuel, manpower hours, and vehicle wear.

**Q7. Is it suitable for municipal smart-city rollouts in India?**  
A: Yes. The solution is designed for ward-scale and city-scale deployments — bin inventory, interactive maps, sensor configuration, predictions, citizen feedback, and command-center operations on one platform.
